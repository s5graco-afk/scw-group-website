Championship-winning Porsche 99X Electric joins the Porsche Museum

Pascal Wehrlein’s world championship-winning Porsche 99X Electric has joined the Porsche Museum collection in Stuttgart. Porsche calls the GEN3/GEN3 Evo racer its most successful single-seater, with six of the ten possible FIA World Championship titles during this Formula E generation.

New Mercedes-Benz GLA enters series production in Rastatt

Mercedes-Benz has started series production of the new GLA at its Rastatt plant. The production system supports both fully electric and 48-volt hybrid versions, while AI, digital twins and MB.OS are integrated into the MO360 manufacturing ecosystem.

Porsche reaches new milestone in high-voltage battery recycling

Porsche has produced pilot battery cells whose cathode active material is made entirely from recycled lithium, nickel, cobalt and manganese recovered from high-voltage batteries. The cells are now undergoing operational testing as Porsche explores a more closed-loop battery supply chain.

Cayenne Electric gains wireless charging and wider customisation

Porsche is expanding the Cayenne Electric for the new model year with options including electrically operated doors, wireless charging at up to 11 kW, a new massage function with 4D audio and Paint to Sample. Porsche says the available palette now reaches 105 paint shades.
