22 SEPTEMBER 2026 · MERCEDES-BENZ

Mercedes-Benz partners with Wayve on camera-based Level 2 driving technology

Mercedes-Benz is working with autonomous-driving technology company Wayve on a camera-based Level 2 driver-assistance system. According to the Financial Times, the technology is planned for integration into at least one Mercedes model within two years. Level 2 remains a supervised system, so the driver stays responsible for the vehicle.

August 2026 EU registration figures put BMW at 45,121 cars, followed by Mercedes-Benz with 40,737 and Audi with 30,626. BMW remained ahead by volume, while Mercedes-Benz recorded the stronger year-over-year increase among the three brands.

Mercedes-Benz expands solid-state battery testing with ProLogium

Mercedes-Benz and ProLogium have signed a joint testing agreement covering ProLogium’s latest Gen4 solid-state battery cells. Mercedes-Benz will receive priority access for electrical, thermal and safety testing as the companies evaluate the technology for potential future vehicle applications.
